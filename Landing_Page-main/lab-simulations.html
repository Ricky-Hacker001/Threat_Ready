Live. Six labs are open in four tracks: Cloud Security, Blue Team, Red Team and Network Security. Every lab runs in a simulated environment; nothing touches real systems.

Lab Simulations

Do the job before you have it.

Role-based tasks that mirror the daily work of a security team — triage, review and remediation — done by you, step by step, from beginner to experienced.

Start free Browse the labs
The format

Perform the work, then see what you missed.

  1. 01 · Brief

    Get the task

    A role-specific task with the evidence a real team would hand you.

  2. 02 · Work

    Work it step by step

    Triage, review or remediate — you make each decision.

  3. 03 · Feedback

    Identified vs missed

    What you caught, what you missed, and why it mattered.

  4. 04 · Progress

    Level up

    Beginner through Expert, with less guidance at each level.
