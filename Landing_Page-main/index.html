FAQ

Questions we hear most.

Anything else: admin@aerovanttech.com

Do I need security experience?

No. Every role track starts at Beginner, and a free account (no card required) is all you need to start practising. Levels then step up through Intermediate, Advanced and Expert.

How is an AI score evaluated fairly?

Every answer goes through three passes — Evaluator, Challenger and Reconciler — against a published rubric with written definitions for every score band. When the passes disagree, the score carries a confidence flag.

You can dispute any score within 14 days and a security engineer reviews it. Read the rubric.

How is my data handled?

Assessment answers are sent to Anthropic's Claude API for scoring; Anthropic does not train models on API inputs. Deleting your account removes your personal data within 30 days.

See the Privacy Policy and Security page.

Can hiring teams use ThreatReady?

Yes. Teams start with a free pilot: we set up role-specific assessments for your candidates and share the scored reports. We respond to pilot requests within one business day. Request a pilot.

What is the difference between the three products?

Attack Reasoning Lab tests how you think through an attack. Lab Simulations test whether you can do the daily work of a role, in hands-on labs. InterviewPrep tests how you perform in an interview built from a real job description and your resume. Compare the products.
