Anatomy of a session

The second question depends on your first answer.

Questions are bounded by the scenario's ATT&CK tactics and the role's competency map. Shallow answers get clarifying follow-ups; strong answers get harder ones.

  1. Q1Opens on the scenario and its architecture.
  2. Q2Follow-up generated from your Q1 answer.
  3. Q3Follow-up generated from your Q2 answer.
  4. Q4Follow-up generated from your Q3 answer.
  5. Q5Follow-up generated from your Q4 answer.

Question categories in the scenario pool include Threat ID, Detection, Architecture, Containment, Remediation, Forensics, Triage and Compliance.

Four levels

Less help, more depth at each level.

LevelTimeHints
Beginner5–8 minFull

Every level runs five adaptive questions.

How it's scored

Each question weights the five dimensions differently.

A "map this to ATT&CK" question leans on Threat Identification; an executive briefing leans on Communication. The weights are published in the rubric.
